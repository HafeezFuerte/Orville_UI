English

1.

The Landlord agrees to lease the property described in this agreement to the Tenant for the lease period stated above.

2.

The Tenant shall pay the agreed rent according to the payment schedule specified in this contract.

3.

Rent payments shall be made on or before the stated due dates through the payment methods accepted by the Landlord.

4.

The Tenant shall maintain the premises in good condition and shall be responsible for damage caused by misuse or negligence.

5.

The property shall be used only for the purpose specified in this lease agreement unless written approval is obtained from the Landlord.

6.

The Tenant shall not sublease, assign, or transfer occupancy of the property without prior written approval from the Landlord.

7.

Utility charges and other consumption-based charges shall be paid by the Tenant unless otherwise stated in this agreement.

8.

The security deposit shall be retained by the Landlord for the duration of the lease and may be used against outstanding dues or damages.

9.

Upon expiry or termination of the lease, the Tenant shall return the property in reasonable condition, subject to normal wear and tear.

10.

Any renewal, cancellation, or amendment of this lease must be agreed in writing by both parties.
